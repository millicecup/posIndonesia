# Stamp-making illustrations

These are illustrative historical examples from the United States, not photographs of Indonesian production or Museum Pos Indonesia equipment. They are bundled for offline viewing. Individual captions in the app identify their context.

The five stages are an educational simplification for a perforated sheet of stamps, not a universal manufacturing specification. Content references:

- Australia Post, [Stamp printing](https://auspost.com.au/about-us/supporting-communities/literacy-education/stamps-collectables/stamp-printing): offset and intaglio methods, proofs, perforation, die-cut self-adhesive stamps, and cutting sheets for retail.
- Library of Congress, [Johnston's documentation of stamp production](https://blogs.loc.gov/picturethis/2019/05/frances-benjamin-johnston-puts-her-stamp-on-documenting-work-at-the-bureau-of-engraving-and-printing/): historical printing, gumming, drying, perforating, trimming and counting. These extra operations demonstrate why five stages are only an overview.
